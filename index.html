About us

Cooked by a family, shared with everyone

Hank’s BBQ began with a young Lebanese family and one simple dream: open a small place of their own and cook the food they love. They started from scratch with very little, and shared every step of the journey on TikTok.

People connected with the honest story and the bold, flavor packed street food, and a community grew around it. We are still a family run kitchen, and every order is made with that same heart.
