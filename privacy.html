How Hank’s BBQ collects, uses and protects your information when you use our website and order with us.

Who we are

Hank’s BBQ is a family run restaurant in Lebanon. This website lets you browse our menu, create an account and order food for delivery or pickup. This policy explains what personal information we collect through the website, why we collect it and what you can do about it.

In short: we only collect what we need to take and deliver your order. We do not sell your information and we do not run advertising trackers on this website.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your full name, phone number and a password. Your password is stored in a protected form, never as plain text.
  • Order details: the items you order, your delivery address, and any notes you write for the kitchen, such as “no pickles”.
  • Order history: the date, status and total of each order, so you can see and cancel your orders.

Information stored on your device

  • A sign in token that keeps you logged in, so you do not have to enter your password on every visit.
  • Your item choices, such as the sauce you picked, so we can remember your preferences.
  • A temporary flag that helps the page transition animation play smoothly.

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and to sign you in.
  • To prepare, deliver and confirm your orders, and to call you if there is a problem with an order or the delivery.
  • To show your order history and let you cancel an order while it is still allowed.
  • To keep the website safe, prevent misuse and fix technical problems.
  • To meet legal or accounting obligations where they apply.

We only use your phone number for matters connected to your account and your orders.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only when it is needed to run the service:

  • Our staff and branches who prepare and deliver your order.
  • Delivery drivers, who see your name, phone number, address and order notes for the delivery.
  • Hosting and technical providers that store the website data on our behalf and are only allowed to use it for that purpose.
  • Authorities, if the law requires us to.

Third party services

To load fonts and icons, this website connects to Google Fonts and cdnjs (Font Awesome). When your browser loads them, those providers can see your IP address and basic browser details, as they would on any website that uses them.

Our Instagram and TikTok links open those platforms, which have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for how they handle your data once you leave our website.

How long we keep it

We keep your account details for as long as your account is open. Order records are kept for as long as we need them to handle questions, refunds and accounting. You can ask us to delete your account at any time. Some order records may be kept longer where the law requires it.

How we protect it

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including protected password storage and limiting access to the people who need it. No website can promise perfect security, so please choose a strong password and do not share it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you,
  • correct information that is wrong,
  • delete your account and personal information, or
  • stop using your information for a particular purpose.

You can also sign out at any time, which removes the sign in token from your device, and clear your browser storage to remove the preferences we saved there. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Children

This website is not meant for children under 16 to create accounts. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page. If a change is important, we will also tell you on the website.
