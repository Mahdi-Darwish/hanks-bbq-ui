Cancellations and problems

You can cancel an order from your account while it is still pending. Once the kitchen has started preparing it, we usually cannot cancel it because the food is cooked to order.

If something is wrong with your order, such as a missing or incorrect item, or a quality problem, contact the branch as soon as possible and we will make it right.
